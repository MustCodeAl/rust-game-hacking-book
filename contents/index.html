137 lessons in 14 chapters, about 67 hours to read and work through the examples. The sequence starts with a game action and works inward to state, bytes, and instructions, then outward to files, messages, and deeper system boundaries. Each lab tests an idea explained beside it; reading in order works best the first time through. Later, use the concept titles and summaries below to find a specific explanation or worked example. Times include the worked exercises; your pace will depend on how much you run and explore.
