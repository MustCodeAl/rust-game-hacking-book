The latest changes to the book, newest first. Each entry says what changed for a reader.

<Updates>
  <Update label="3 October 2026" tags="Progress, Hover cards, Reader mode" description="Keep track of what you have read, and get help without leaving the page.">

  - **Mark lessons and chapters done.** A tick appears beside each finished lesson in the lesson list, each chapter shows how many of its lessons are done, and the contents page and the home cards show the same progress. It is saved in your browser only.
  - **Hide either side panel from the panel itself.** The lesson list and "On this page" each have a Hide button, and a tab at the screen edge brings the panel back.
  - **Hover cards, used sparingly.** A glossary word shows its definition when you hover, focus, or tap it, but only where the book first uses the word, not every time it appears. A mention such as "Lesson 2.1" is now a link that shows what that lesson covers. Lessons can also write cards of their own: an example, a reference, a tip, an alternative, a recommendation, or a closer explanation, each labelled by kind. The Reader theme panel has a Hover cards On/Off choice if you would rather not see them.
  - **Reader mode keeps its controls on screen.** Play, pause, stop, and a way out of reader mode stay at the top while you scroll, and diagram labels are no longer read with their words run together.
  - **Lesson components.** Steps, tabs, accordions, fields, maths, and more are now available to every lesson. The [components page](/components/) shows each one.
  - **A chat button** for asking questions about the book appears on every page. Its hint text names the lesson, chapter, or section you are reading, and it takes the chapter's colour. Move it to any corner, or turn it off, in the Reader theme panel.

  </Update>
  <Update label="2 October 2026" tags="Reader, Colour" description="Calmer, more consistent themes, and better listening and printing.">

  - Reader themes blend their colours more consistently, and each theme's reading colours follow its own palette.
  - Diagram playback is clearer, every lesson has a listening edition, and a single chapter can be printed on its own.

  </Update>
  <Update label="1 October 2026" tags="Diagrams, Reader" description="Animated diagrams and reader appearance controls.">

  - Animated walkthroughs now cover 42 lessons, three in every chapter. Each can be played, stepped through, or read in full, and respects your reduced-motion setting.
  - The reader theme panel gained controls for diagram backgrounds, text size, spacing, and heading style.

  </Update>
  <Update label="30 September 2026" tags="Lessons, Colour" description="A firmware lesson, deeper fundamentals, and colour with a meaning.">

  - A new lesson on firmware and bare-metal Rust, and deeper explanations of game fundamentals.
  - Chapters were rebalanced and reader spacing improved.
  - Every colour now has a documented role, and the same colour always means the same thing.

  </Update>
  <Update label="29 September 2026" tags="Lessons" description="The chapters were reordered so each idea follows the ones it needs.">

  - The later chapters were reordered around what each lesson needs from the ones before it.
  - A Rust primer was added, and the whole book was revised as a guide, a reference, and a tutorial.

  </Update>
  <Update label="28 September 2026" tags="Lessons" description="The teaching progression was revised.">

  - Lessons now introduce each idea before they use it, and the opening chapters build up from visual foundations to games and hacking fundamentals.

  </Update>
</Updates>
