This page is a map, not a lesson. Every part of a game that the lessons touch is
listed here with one sentence on what it is and a link to the section that
explains how it works. Each section is written for someone who has not seen the
idea before, uses real numbers, and ends in what you would see if you looked at
the running game from outside.

Most of the parts below are what an engine supplies (Lesson 4.1). The small
example programs in the
[Bevy engine's example gallery](https://bevy.org/examples/) show nearly every one
of them in a few dozen lines, if you would like to see one running.

## The program and the world it runs

- **Application.** The program that starts up, loads its settings, installs its
  parts, and runs the loop. [Starting up](/pages/1/11/#starting-up-plugins-and-settings),
  [one frame](/pages/1/11/#one-frame-step-by-step).
- **Window.** What the game draws into: its size, scale, fullscreen mode, refresh,
  and what changes when there is more than one. [The window itself](/pages/8/06/#the-window-itself).
- **Movement.** Changing a position over time, with the time step the engine
  uses. [Movement needs space and time](/pages/1/03/#movement-needs-space-and-time),
  [time inside the update](/pages/1/11/#time-inside-the-update),
  [real time, game time, and the clamp](/pages/1/11/#real-time-game-time-and-the-clamp).
- **ECS (entity component system).** Entities as identities, components as data,
  and systems as the code that runs over them.
  [Entities, components, and systems](/pages/1/11/#entities-components-and-systems),
  [how components sit in memory](/pages/3/05/#component-pools-store-fields-in-separate-collections),
  [tables and sparse sets](/pages/4/12/#how-components-are-stored-tables-and-sparse-sets).
- **Resources, events, and queries.** How systems share data without calling one
  another, and how they ask for only the entities they need.
  [Three ways systems share data](/pages/4/12/#three-ways-systems-share-data),
  [events](/pages/4/12/#events-messages-with-a-short-life),
  [queries](/pages/4/12/#queries-asking-for-entities-by-what-they-have),
  [change detection](/pages/4/12/#change-detection-do-work-only-for-what-changed).
- **Schedules, order, and states.** Which system runs first, which can run at the same
  time, and which are skipped.
  [A frame is a fixed list of schedules](/pages/4/12/#a-frame-is-a-fixed-list-of-schedules),
  [order and parallel work](/pages/4/12/#order-and-parallel-work-when-a-system-may-run),
  [run conditions and states](/pages/4/12/#run-conditions-and-states),
  [commands](/pages/4/12/#commands-wait-for-a-safe-moment).
- **Input.** From device events to actions: buttons and their edges, sticks and dead
  zones, keys, the mouse, touch, and controllers.
  [How a game reads input](/pages/8/11/),
  [why edges go missing](/pages/8/11/#why-edges-go-missing-in-fixed-steps).
- **Scene.** A group of entities, often a hierarchy, loaded as one.
  [Scenes](/pages/1/11/#scenes-a-world-you-can-load).
- **Games.** The parts assembled into one small game, with its states.
  [A whole game from these parts](/pages/1/11/#a-whole-game-from-these-parts-breakout).
- **Async tasks.** Slow work done off the main thread and returned through a
  channel. [Slow work leaves the frame](/pages/1/11/#slow-work-leaves-the-frame),
  [worker threads and tasks](/pages/10/06/#worker-threads-and-tasks-keeping-slow-work-off-the-frame).
- **Usage.** Everyday patterns such as timers and cooldowns, and popups such as
  a context menu. [Cooldowns](/pages/1/11/#time-inside-the-update),
  [popups](/pages/8/09/#what-a-games-interface-is-made-of).

## Space, cameras, and math

- **Math.** Vectors, blending between values, easing, curves, bounding shapes,
  turns, and random points.
  [Coordinates, vectors, and directions](/pages/4/11/),
  [blending values](/pages/4/11/#blending-values-interpolation-easing-and-following),
  [extrapolation](/pages/4/11/#past-the-two-samples-extrapolation),
  [bounding volumes](/pages/4/11/#bounding-volumes-distance-as-a-contact-test),
  [quaternions](/pages/5/01/#turning-without-gimbal-lock-quaternions),
  [random spread](/pages/5/07/#random-spread-choosing-a-point-in-a-circle).
- **Transforms.** Placing, turning, and scaling an object, a child on its parent,
  and the local and world copies an engine keeps of each.
  [Transforms](/pages/5/01/#transforms-placing-an-object-in-the-world),
  [two copies of a transform](/pages/5/01/#two-copies-of-a-transform),
  [four engines, four conventions](/pages/5/01/#four-engines-four-conventions).
- **Camera.** What is seen: perspective and orthographic views, several cameras with
  their own viewports, and the ways a camera moves.
  [Field of view and aspect ratio](/pages/5/01/#what-field-of-view-and-aspect-ratio-change),
  [orthographic projection](/pages/5/01/#orthographic-projection-no-shrinking-with-distance),
  [several cameras](/pages/5/01/#several-cameras-one-world),
  [shake, follow, orbit, and zoom](/pages/5/07/#camera-motion-shake-follow-orbit-and-zoom).
- **Picking.** Finding which object is under the cursor.
  [Picking](/pages/5/05/#picking-the-same-ray-from-the-cursor).

## Drawing

- **2D rendering.** Sprites, sprite sheets, tilemaps, layers, and transparency.
  [Two-dimensional rendering](/pages/5/02/#two-dimensional-rendering-uses-the-same-pipeline).
- **3D rendering.** Meshes, materials, light, shadows, fog, visibility, culling, high
  dynamic range, anti-aliasing, and the passes that make up a frame.
  [Materials, textures, and light](/pages/5/02/#materials-textures-and-light),
  [transparency](/pages/5/02/#transparency-and-draw-order),
  [culling](/pages/5/02/#skipping-what-cannot-be-seen),
  [who decides whether an object is drawn](/pages/5/02/#who-decides-whether-an-object-is-drawn),
  [passes](/pages/5/02/#a-frame-is-several-passes),
  [HDR and anti-aliasing](/pages/5/02/#wider-colours-and-smoother-edges),
  [the renderer's own copy of the world](/pages/5/02/#the-renderer-works-from-its-own-copy),
  [texture coordinates](/pages/5/01/#texture-coordinates-the-same-top-or-bottom-choice).
- **Shaders.** The small GPU programs that turn data into pixels, and what they
  read. [What a shader reads](/pages/5/03/#what-a-shader-reads).
- **Shaders, advanced.** Variants, instancing, compute shaders, and full-screen
  effects.
  [Variants, instancing, and shaders that do not draw](/pages/5/03/#variants-instancing-and-shaders-that-do-not-draw).
- **Animation.** Keyframes, skeletons, blending, and morph targets.
  [How animation moves a character](/pages/5/06/#how-animation-moves-a-character).
- **UI (user interface).** Text, layout, scaling, nine-slice art, clipping, input,
  and focus.
  [What a game's interface is made of](/pages/8/09/#what-a-games-interface-is-made-of).

## Files and sound

- **Assets.** How a file becomes something the game can draw or play, how long it
  stays loaded, and when a changed file is picked up.
  [Loading an asset](/pages/9/01/#from-a-file-to-something-on-screen-loading-an-asset),
  [how long an asset lives](/pages/9/01/#how-long-an-asset-lives),
  [events and reloading](/pages/9/01/#events-and-reloading).
- **glTF.** An open format for 3D models, read link by link.
  [Reading a glTF file](/pages/9/03/#models-reading-a-gltf-file).
- **Audio.** Samples, mixing, pitch, and where a sound comes from.
  [Sounds](/pages/9/03/#sounds-samples-mixing-and-position),
  [two ears and falloff](/pages/9/03/#two-ears-and-falloff).

## Looking at a running game

- **Diagnostics.** Frame time, logs, and the difference a debug build makes to
  every measurement. [Measuring the frame](/pages/1/11/#measuring-the-frame).
- **Dev tools and tools.** Overlays, editors, and viewers.
  [Overlays, gizmos, editors, and stress tests](/pages/1/11/#overlays-gizmos-editors-and-stress-tests).
- **Gizmos.** Shapes drawn for one frame to show what is invisible.
  [Gizmos](/pages/5/09/#gizmos-the-same-projection-used-by-developers).
- **Stress tests.** Loads that find where a game slows down.
  [Why many small draws cost frame time](/pages/5/03/#why-many-small-draws-cost-frame-time).
